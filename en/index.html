Frequently asked questions about our Fès services.
Clear answers.

The technical essentials to know before starting a print, cut-vinyl or signage project.

Do you provide on-site installation?+
We focus on design, precision cutting and ready-to-apply preparation to ensure a clean, accurately aligned result. Each piece is delivered weeded, with transfer tape and simple application guidance. If your project requires professional on-site installation, it can be handled easily by a local installer.
What do I receive with a vinyl project?+
A ready-to-apply piece: artwork prepared for production, plotter-cut vinyl, unwanted material removed, and transfer tape applied so letters and shapes stay perfectly aligned during installation.
How long does production take?+
Timing depends on size, quantity, design complexity and material. A clear production time is confirmed before work begins; urgent requests can be reviewed directly on WhatsApp.
Which vinyl and materials do you use? Are they suitable outdoors?+
Material is selected for the use: permanent vinyl, window vinyl, frosted film or an exterior-suitable option. For projects exposed to sun and rain, we prioritize grades designed for that environment. The exact brand and grade are confirmed before production so we never promise durability the selected material does not guarantee.
Do you only work in Fès?+
MAQTA is based in Fès. Local collection or handover can be arranged in Fès, and shipping to other Moroccan cities can be considered according to the size, material and nature of the order.

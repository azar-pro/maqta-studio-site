Frequently asked.
Clearly answered.

Clear answers about branding, print, signage, websites, e-commerce and mobile applications before you start your project.

01

Branding & design

Everything that builds the visual foundation of your brand.

Do you design logos only or complete visual identities?+

Both. MAQTA can create a standalone logo when the need is specific, or build a broader identity with color palette, typography, visual rules and brand applications. The exact scope is defined in the quote before work begins.

What can a visual identity include?+

Depending on the project: primary logo and variants, color palette, typography, graphic system, a compact usage guide and useful applications such as business cards, packaging, social media or signage.

Can you design only a business card?+

Yes. A business card can be designed as a standalone project, single- or double-sided. If printing is also required, paper, finish and quantity are quoted separately so production matches the intended use.

How many revisions are included in a design project?+

The number of correction rounds depends on the project and is confirmed before work starts. The goal is to make approvals clear from the brief so unnecessary back-and-forth is avoided.

02

Print, signage & vinyl

Production, materials and preparation for physical applications.

What printed materials can you prepare?+

Business cards, labels, stickers, menus, promotional pieces, packaging and other formats can be designed and prepared for production. Material, finish and quantity are confirmed before fabrication.

What will I receive for a vinyl project?+

A ready-to-apply element: prepared artwork, plotter-cut vinyl, excess material weeded away and transfer film applied to keep letters and shapes correctly aligned during application.

Do you provide on-site installation?+

We focus on design, precision cutting and ready-to-apply preparation. Each element is delivered weeded, with transfer film and simple application guidance. When professional installation is needed, it can be handled easily by a local installer.

Does the vinyl resist sun and rain?+

The material is selected for the intended use: permanent, window, frosted or exterior grade. For sun and rain exposure, we select a grade intended for that environment. The exact brand and material grade are confirmed before production.

Can you prepare graphics for a car or business vehicle?+

Yes. We can design the graphics, adapt the composition to the vehicle zones and prepare elements for cutting or production. Vehicle dimensions and reference photos are checked before final artwork is prepared.

03

Websites & apps

Landing pages, business websites, e-commerce and mobile experiences.

What types of websites do you build?+

Depending on the goal: landing pages, showcase websites, portfolios, multi-page business websites or e-commerce stores. The structure is chosen around the real objective: present, generate enquiries, sell or strengthen the brand online.

What is the difference between a landing page and a showcase website?+

A landing page focuses on one main objective within a single page, such as generating enquiries or presenting an offer. A showcase website uses multiple pages to explain the company, services, work and information in greater depth.

Do you build e-commerce stores?+

Yes. A project can include catalogue, product pages, cart, checkout journey and mobile adaptation. Payment, delivery, product management and required integrations are defined before the quote.

Can you handle hosting and the domain name?+

Deployment, hosting and domain configuration can be included or guided depending on the project. Any recurring third-party service fees are presented separately and clearly before approval.

Do you also build web apps or client portals?+

Yes. Depending on the need, a project can include a client portal, dashboard, advanced form or custom web-based tool. The scope is defined around users, data and required functions.

Do you design mobile applications?+

Yes, when the need genuinely calls for an app rather than a website. Cost and timeline depend on screens, features, user accounts, data, notifications, payments and target platforms, so a functional brief is required before estimating.

Will the website or app work well on phones?+

Yes. Interfaces are designed for the relevant screen sizes. Responsive mobile behavior is part of website design and integration; for an application, the target platforms and devices are defined in the brief.

04

Project, budget & delivery

What to know before sending your project request.

How long does a project take?+

Timing depends on complexity, approvals, content and any physical production. A business card, full identity, e-commerce site and mobile app naturally require different schedules. A clear estimate is provided after the brief.

How is pricing calculated?+

Pricing depends on the real scope: number of deliverables or pages, level of customization, features, quantity, material and production when applicable. The contact form lets you share a budget range, but that field is optional.

What do you need from me to start?+

Ideally: your activity, project goal, required deliverables, available content, dimensions where relevant, a few references if you have them and your deadline. If information is missing, we clarify it together.

Do you only work in Fès?+

MAQTA is based in Fès. Design and digital projects can be handled remotely. For physical items, local collection, handover or shipping to other Moroccan cities can be considered depending on size and order type.
